# EasyRemote privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-08_

EasyRemote lets you control your own computer from your phone over your local network.

**We collect nothing.** The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting service, and it sends no data to the developer or to any third party. There is no EasyRemote server.

**What the app stores, on your phone only:** the paired computers (name, address, certificate fingerprint and a pairing token) and your settings. You can remove a computer in the app, and uninstalling the app deletes everything.

**What the app sends:** touchpad movements, key presses, volume and media commands, directly from your phone to the EasyRemote agent on your own computer, over an encrypted (TLS) connection on your Wi-Fi. The connection only works with a computer you paired with.

**Permissions:** Internet / network state (to reach your computer on the local network). The QR scanner is provided by Google Play services and the app itself does not access the camera or any photos.

**Contact:** open an issue at https://github.com/BuTema/EasyRemote/issues
